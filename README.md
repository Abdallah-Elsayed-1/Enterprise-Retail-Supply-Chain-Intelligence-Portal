## Executive Summary

**Enterprise Retail & Supply Chain Intelligence Portal** is an end-to-end Microsoft Power BI decision system analyzing **$2,261,537** in gross revenue across **4,922** orders, **9,800** sold items, and **793** customers across **4 US regions** (2015–2018).

Engineered on a normalized **Star Schema** data model with custom **GPU-accelerated HTML/CSS/SVG visuals**, the solution unifies commercial revenue tracking, supply chain **SLA compliance (`67.5%`)**, delay risk exposure (**`$697,956`** Revenue at Risk), and customer retention economics (**`81.9%`** Repeat Loyalty Share) into a cohesive executive command center.

### Key Supply Chain & SLA Insights
- **SLA Compliance Deficit (`-17.5%` Gap vs. `85.0%` Target):** Overall on-time fulfillment stands at **`67.5%`** (**`3,324`** on-time orders vs. **`1,598`** late orders, a **`32.5%`** defect rate) with an average shipping cycle of **`4.0 days`** and an average breach delay of **`1.3 days`** (exceeding the `1.0 day` max tolerance).
- **Volume-Induced SLA Degradation:** As annual order volume scaled by **`+75.4%`** from **`947`** orders (2015) to **`1,661`** orders (2018), on-time fulfillment slipped from **`68.8%`** (2016 peak) down to **`66.3%`** in 2018.
- **Shipping Mode Bottlenecks:**
  - **Second Class** exhibits the worst SLA compliance at **`58.6%`** (**`391`** late out of **`944`** orders, **`$172.8K`** at risk), followed closely by **First Class** at **`60.1%`** (**`308`** late out of **`772`** orders, **`$121.8K`** at risk).
  - **Standard Class** carries the highest financial exposure at **`$399.9K`** (**`887`** late orders, **`69.9%`** SLA), whereas **Same Day** achieves **`95.4%`** compliance (**`249`** on-time vs. only **`12`** late orders, **`$3.5K`** exposure).
- **Category Delay Exposure:** **Office Supplies** leads delayed revenue exposure at **`$247K`**, followed by **Technology (`$230K`)** and **Furniture (`$221K`)**.

## 7. Data Engineering & Star Schema Architecture

### Power Query M-Code ETL Pipeline

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://i.postimg.cc/xCGxR8wx/Screenshot-2026-11-20-085958.png" alt="Power Query ETL Pipeline" width="100%" />
</div>

- **Data Cleansing & Profiling:** Standardized **`9,800`** raw records (`100%` valid across all columns) by handling missing postal codes, zero-padding 5-digit ZIP codes, enforcing strict column data types, and trimming text fields.
- **Operational Feature Engineering:** Engineered `Shipping Days` (`Ship Date - Order Date`), mapped delivery-tier `SLA Target Days`, classified row-level `SLA Status` (`On-Time` vs. `Late`), and generated surrogate dimension keys (`GeoKey`, `ProductKey`).

### Star Schema Dimensional Model

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://i.postimg.cc/Twj791zy/Screenshot-2026-11-20-085448.png" alt="Star Schema Data Model" width="100%" />
</div>

- **Fact Table (`Fact_Sales`):** Line-item transaction grain storing `Sales`, `Shipping Days`, `SLA Target Days`, `SLA Status`, and foreign keys (`Order ID`, `Customer ID`, `ProductKey`, `GeoKey`, `Order Date`, `Ship Date`).
- **Dimension Tables (`5`):** `Dim_Date` (active relationship on `Order Date`, inactive relationship on `Ship Date` for `USERELATIONSHIP` delivery analysis), `Dim_Customer` (enriched with `First Purchase Date` for New vs. Repeat cohort modeling), `Dim_Product`, `Dim_Geography`, and `Dim_Orders`.
- **Modular DAX Architecture:** Cleanly separated into **`Dax Measures`** (core business KPIs, time intelligence, SLA & cohort logic) and **`HTML DAX MEASURES`** (GPU-accelerated SVG/CSS visual rendering).
